# Installing and Launching Power Automate Desktop

Power Automate Desktop (PAD) lets you automate tasks on your computer — clicking buttons, typing into forms, or moving files. It is free for Windows 10 and 11 users.

## System Requirements

- **Operating system** — Windows 10 or 11 (some Windows Server versions also work).
- **Hardware** — No special hardware required. If your computer runs Office or Teams without slowing down, it can handle PAD (4–8 GB of RAM and a decent processor is more than enough).
- **Admin access** — Needed to install the app and its browser extensions.
- **Internet connection** — Needed to sign in and use cloud features.
- **Browser extensions** — Required if you plan to automate tasks in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox (e.g., logging into a website and downloading a report).

## Installing PAD

1. Go to the [download page](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2102613).
2. Download the installer.
3. Run the installer — open the `.exe` file and follow the setup steps.
4. Sign in with your Microsoft work or personal account. Signing in links your desktop flows to your Power Automate cloud account, so you can monitor or trigger them remotely if needed.

## Launching PAD

After signing in, the Home screen shows your saved desktop flows and folders. From here:

1. Click **New Flow** to start a new one.
2. Name your flow and begin building in the visual editor.

**Example:** Create a flow that opens a legacy desktop app, logs in, pulls a report, and saves it to a shared folder — all without manual steps.
