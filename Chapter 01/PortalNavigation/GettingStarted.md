# Navigating the Power Automate Portal

The Power Automate portal acts as your central workspace — where you create flows, manage them, monitor performance, and collaborate with others. Whether it's a simple notification or a complex enterprise-level workflow, this web interface offers the structure and tools needed to build and maintain automations effectively.

## Getting Started as an Individual

Starting with Power Automate is simple, even if you are new to Microsoft's ecosystem. All you need is an email address:

1. Head over to [https://make.powerautomate.com/](https://make.powerautomate.com/).
2. Enter your email address in the sign-in portion and click **Next**.
3. If you haven't used Microsoft online services with that email before, spend a few moments completing the setup.

With that, you're ready to begin building your first automation.

## Portal Areas

Once signed in, the home screen gives you a clear view of where to find what you need:

- **Home** — View recent flows, templates, and key shortcuts.
- **Create** — The workspace where ideas turn into automation. Scheduled jobs, instant triggers, or complex business processes — all flow types are created here.
- **My flows** — A personal dashboard for everything you have built or that has been shared with you. Drafts, running flows, and errors all live here.
- **Approvals** — A centralized view to track, manage, and respond to approval requests, sent or received, without digging through emails.
- **Templates** — Pre-built flow recipes crafted to solve real-world scenarios — plug, tweak, and play. Ideal for quick wins.
- **Learn** — Tutorials, training modules, and events to keep your skills sharp, for beginners and experienced makers alike.
