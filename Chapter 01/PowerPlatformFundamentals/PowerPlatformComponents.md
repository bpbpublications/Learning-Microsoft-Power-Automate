# Power Platform Fundamentals

Power Automate on its own is a robust automation tool, but its real potential shines when it works in tandem with the rest of the Power Platform suite. It is one of five core applications that together form the backbone of Microsoft's low-code/no-code ecosystem.

## The Five Core Applications

- **Power Apps** — Build and share apps within your organization that work smoothly across browsers and mobile devices. Two key flavors: **canvas apps** (drag-and-drop design control) and **model-driven apps** (structured, data-relationship driven).
- **Power BI** — A business analytics solution offering interactive data visualization to help you visualize and share data and insights across your organization.
- **Power Pages** — The go-to platform when external users, like customers or partners, need to interact with your systems through a professional web portal. Low-code for quick launches, extensible by developers for more complex needs.
- **Power Virtual Agents** — Build chatbots that handle routine questions and tasks with no coding required. Connects seamlessly with Power Automate to trigger flows based on user input in chat — bots can create tickets, send emails, and take other actions, not just reply.
- **Power Automate** — The automation engine that connects the other applications and external services, letting tasks run in the background without constant human intervention.

Power Platform is a group of tools that helps you build apps, automate tasks, understand data, and create chatbots — without needing to write much code.

## Types of Flows in Power Automate

Power Automate offers different types of flows, each designed for a specific kind of automation. Choosing the right flow depends on the nature of the task — cloud-based, desktop-focused, or tied to a structured business process.

| Flow type | Suited for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud flows | Automating tasks across apps | Saving email attachments to OneDrive |
| Desktop flows | Robotic process automation (RPA) / automating UI-based tasks | Data entry in legacy software |
| Business process flows | Guiding users through structured, stage-based business processes | Employee onboarding approvals |
