# Understanding Environments, Solutions, and Licensing Basics

It's helpful to understand three things — environments, solutions, and licensing — before you start building flows in Power Automate. These are not complicated, but they do affect how you work.

## Environments

An environment is a workspace that holds your flows, apps, and data.

- If you are using Power Automate on your own, you are probably in the default environment.
- Companies usually set up separate environments for development, testing, and production.
- Some features, like Dataverse, only work in certain environments with the right license.
- You need admin access to create or manage environments — see the [Power Platform admin center](https://admin.powerplatform.microsoft.com).

## Solutions

As automation efforts grow, keeping things organized becomes essential. **Solutions** group things together — flows, apps, connections, and more.

- Helpful when working on a project with multiple parts.
- Make it easier to move work between environments.
- Not needed for small tasks, but valuable for team projects or anything going live.

## Licensing

Power Automate has different plans depending on what you need:

| Plan | Good for | Connector type included |
|---|---|---|
| Free / M365 | Personal use, simple flows | Standard connectors |
| Per user | Regular users with complex flows | Premium connectors, attended RPA |
| Per flow | Organization-wide automation | Shared flows, unattended RPA |
