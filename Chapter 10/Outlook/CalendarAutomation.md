# Outlook Calendar Automation

## Overview

Calendar automation can create, update, and manage meetings or reminders based on business events.

## Typical Pattern

```text
Business Event
     ↓
Check Date / Participants
     ↓
Create or Update Event
     ↓
Send Notification
```

## Common Scenarios

- Schedule follow-up meetings.
- Create calendar events from forms.
- Send meeting reminders.
- Update appointments when a business status changes.

## Best Practices

- Validate dates and time zones.
- Avoid creating duplicate events.
- Confirm participant information.
- Keep meeting subjects meaningful.
- Test calendar permissions before production deployment.
