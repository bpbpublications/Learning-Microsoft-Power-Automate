# Teams Approvals

## Overview

Teams can provide a convenient interface for users to review and respond to approval requests generated by Power Automate.

## Approval Flow

```text
Business Request
      ↓
Start Approval
      ↓
Teams Notification
      ↓
Approve / Reject
      ↓
Update Business Record
```

## Design Considerations

- Define the approver clearly.
- Handle rejection and timeout paths.
- Prevent duplicate requests.
- Record the decision and timestamp where appropriate.
- Validate the response before changing business state.
