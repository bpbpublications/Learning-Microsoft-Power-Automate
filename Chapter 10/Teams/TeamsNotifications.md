# Teams Notifications

## Overview

Power Automate can send targeted notifications to Teams when business events occur.

## Typical Pattern

```text
Business Event
      ↓
Evaluate Condition
      ↓
Create Notification
      ↓
Teams
```

## Common Scenarios

- Notify a team about an approval request.
- Report flow failures.
- Announce business events.
- Send status updates.

## Best Practices

- Send messages only when they add value.
- Use clear and concise content.
- Avoid excessive notifications.
- Choose the appropriate team or channel.
- Do not expose confidential information unnecessarily.
