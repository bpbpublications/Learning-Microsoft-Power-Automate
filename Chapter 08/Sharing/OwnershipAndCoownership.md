# Flow Ownership and Co-Ownership

## Overview

Clear ownership helps ensure that an automation has accountable people responsible for maintenance, support, and business continuity.

## Ownership Model

For important flows, document:

- Business owner
- Technical owner
- Support contact
- Environment
- Business criticality

## Co-Owners

Co-owners can help maintain a flow when more than one person is responsible for its operation.

Grant co-owner access only when the person requires the associated responsibilities.

## Business Continuity

Avoid designs where a critical automation depends on one person's knowledge or personal account.

Document:

- Purpose of the flow
- Dependencies
- Connections
- Support procedure
- Recovery process

## Review

Review ownership when:

- A team member changes role.
- A flow moves to another support team.
- A process becomes business-critical.
- A connection or dependency changes.

## Security

Grant only the access required for the assigned responsibility. Do not share personal credentials to transfer ownership.
