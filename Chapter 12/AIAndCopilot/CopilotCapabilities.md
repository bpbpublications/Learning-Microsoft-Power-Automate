# Copilot Capabilities in Automation

## Overview

Copilot can assist with creating, explaining, modifying, and understanding automation. Its role should be treated as an accelerator rather than a replacement for design review and testing.

## Common Uses

- Describe an automation requirement in natural language.
- Explain an existing flow.
- Suggest expressions or actions.
- Help summarize automation logic.
- Assist with troubleshooting ideas.

## Human Review

Generated suggestions should be reviewed before they are used in a production process.

```text
Copilot Suggestion
       ↓
Human Review
       ↓
Test
       ↓
Validate Business Result
       ↓
Deploy
```

## Governance

Consider data sensitivity, access permissions, output accuracy, and audit requirements when using AI assistance.
