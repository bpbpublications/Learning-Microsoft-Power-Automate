# Responsible AI Principles

## Overview

Responsible AI focuses on using AI in a way that is appropriate for the business context, transparent enough to review, and controlled according to risk.

## Core Principles

- Human oversight
- Security and privacy
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Reliability
- Appropriate use

## Automation Context

For an AI-enabled flow, identify:

1. What the AI does.
2. What data it receives.
3. What decision or output it produces.
4. What happens if the output is wrong.
5. Who is accountable for the business outcome.

## Risk-Based Controls

Higher-impact use cases generally require stronger validation, approval, monitoring, and audit controls.
