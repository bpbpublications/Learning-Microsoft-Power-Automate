# Process Discovery and Automation Opportunities

## Overview

Process discovery uses process data to understand how work is actually performed and where automation opportunities may exist.

## Discovery Flow

```text
Process Data
   ↓
Process Discovery
   ↓
Identify Variations
   ↓
Find Bottlenecks
   ↓
Assess Automation Opportunity
   ↓
Prioritize
```

## What to Look For

- Repetitive activities
- Manual data entry
- Long waiting times
- Rework
- Frequent exceptions
- High-volume transactions

## Automation Assessment

Not every process variation should be automated. Consider business value, stability, risk, data quality, and expected maintenance effort.
