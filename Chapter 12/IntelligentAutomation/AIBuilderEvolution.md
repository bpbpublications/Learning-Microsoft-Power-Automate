# Intelligent Automation Evolution

## Overview

Intelligent automation combines workflow automation with capabilities such as document processing, language understanding, classification, and prediction.

## Evolution

```text
Task Automation
      ↓
Workflow Automation
      ↓
RPA + Cloud Automation
      ↓
AI-Assisted Automation
      ↓
Intelligent Automation
```

## Practical Design

Use AI where it adds value, such as:

- Extracting information from unstructured documents
- Classifying incoming requests
- Summarizing content
- Identifying sentiment
- Supporting human decisions

## Human-in-the-Loop

For decisions with business or compliance impact, consider human review rather than treating AI output as automatically authoritative.
