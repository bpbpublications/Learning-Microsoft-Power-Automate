# Data Loss Prevention Policies

## Overview

Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies help control how connectors can be used together and reduce the risk of inappropriate data movement.

## Governance Purpose

DLP controls should align with the organization's data-classification and security requirements.

Typical connector groupings include:

- Business data connectors
- Non-business data connectors
- Blocked connectors

## Design Considerations

Before changing a DLP policy, assess:

- Existing flows that use affected connectors.
- Business impact.
- Exceptions that may be required.
- Testing requirements.
- Communication and change-management needs.

## Operational Practice

DLP policies should be reviewed periodically and changes should follow the organization's governance and approval process.
