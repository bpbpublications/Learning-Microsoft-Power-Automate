# CoE Governance Controls

## Overview

A Center of Excellence (CoE) can define standards and controls that help an organization scale Power Platform responsibly.

## Typical Controls

A governance model may cover:

- Environment strategy
- Maker onboarding
- Solution standards
- Ownership
- Security and DLP
- Monitoring
- Support
- Lifecycle management

## Governance Pattern

```text
Standards
   ↓
Enable Makers
   ↓
Monitor Usage
   ↓
Identify Risk
   ↓
Improve Controls
```

## Balance

Governance should reduce unnecessary risk without creating barriers that prevent legitimate automation delivery.

Controls should be risk-based and reviewed as the platform and organizational needs change.
