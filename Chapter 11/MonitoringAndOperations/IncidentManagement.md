# Incident Management

## Overview

Incident management provides a repeatable approach for handling automation failures that affect business operations.

## Incident Flow

```text
Detect
 ↓
Classify
 ↓
Assess Impact
 ↓
Restore Service
 ↓
Identify Root Cause
 ↓
Document
 ↓
Prevent Recurrence
```

## Classification

Consider severity, business impact, affected processes, duration, and whether a workaround exists.

## Root Cause

Do not stop at restarting a failed flow. Determine whether the underlying cause was configuration, permissions, dependency failure, data quality, capacity, or application behavior.

## Lessons Learned

For recurring or significant incidents, document the cause, resolution, preventive action, and owner.
