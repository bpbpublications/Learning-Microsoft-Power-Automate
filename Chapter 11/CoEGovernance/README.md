## Governance Flow

```text
Define Standards
      ↓
Assess Automation
      ↓
Approve and Govern
      ↓
Monitor Usage
      ↓
Review Risk
      ↓
Improve Controls
```

## Key Principle

Governance should enable safe automation while keeping controls proportional to business risk and platform scale.
