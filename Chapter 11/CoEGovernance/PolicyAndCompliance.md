# Policy and Compliance

## Overview

Enterprise automation policies define boundaries for secure use of connectors, data, environments, and applications.

## Policy Areas

- Data protection
- Connector usage
- Access control
- Environment restrictions
- Audit requirements
- Retention and lifecycle

## Policy Pattern

```text
Business Requirement
       ↓
Risk Assessment
       ↓
Policy Decision
       ↓
Implementation
       ↓
Monitoring
       ↓
Review
```

## Practical Guidance

Policies should be documented in terms that makers and support teams can apply consistently.

## Exceptions

Exceptions should have a documented owner, business justification, risk assessment, approval, and review or expiry date.
