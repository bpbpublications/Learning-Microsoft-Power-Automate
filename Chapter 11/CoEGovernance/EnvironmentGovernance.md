# Environment Governance

## Overview

Environment governance defines how Power Platform environments are created, secured, used, and reviewed.

## Governance Controls

Define:

- Environment purpose
- Owners
- Allowed makers
- Security roles
- Connector policies
- Lifecycle and review requirements

## Environment Lifecycle

```text
Request
 ↓
Approve
 ↓
Create
 ↓
Configure
 ↓
Operate
 ↓
Review
 ↓
Retire
```

## Production Controls

Production environments normally require stronger access restrictions, controlled deployment, and defined support ownership.

## Review

Periodically review unused environments, privileged access, business ownership, and policy compliance.
