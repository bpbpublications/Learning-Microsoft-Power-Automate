# CoE Governance Framework

## Overview

A Center of Excellence (CoE) governance framework establishes standards for secure, consistent, and supportable automation.

## Governance Areas

- Environment management
- Security and access
- DLP and connector controls
- Solution standards
- Ownership
- Monitoring
- Lifecycle management

## Governance Cycle

```text
Define Standards
 ↓
Enable Makers
 ↓
Monitor Adoption
 ↓
Review Compliance
 ↓
Improve Standards
```

## Balance

Governance should reduce risk without creating unnecessary barriers to appropriate automation.
